# Home

## Welcome to County Island Community

We are neighbors in the 13 unincorporated county islands within the Peoria Metro Planning Area (Peoria MPA), Arizona.

Our islands have no local government looking out for them. We are not part of the city, and we are a small corner of a very large county. So we look out for each other.

We want to keep our community safe, secure, and connected. And we want to stay a county island.

**Our mission:** to promote safety, security, and general community within the county islands.

### What we care about
- **Health & Safety:** reliable fire and EMS coverage for every home.
- **Tax Reform:** clear answers about city sales tax charged to island residents.
- **Water:** dependable, fair water service.
- **Staying a County Island:** our choice, our voice.

# About

## Who we are

County Island Community (CIC) is a loosely organized, representative group of residents from the 13 county islands in the Peoria MPA.

We live inside Peoria's planning area, but we are not part of the city. No city council speaks for us, and no local body is focused on our needs.

CIC exists to fill that gap. Neighbors speak up together so every island is heard.

CIC is [ENTITY STATUS].

## Our area

There are 13 county islands, each with its own geography. Together they hold about [HOUSEHOLD COUNT] households. [See the islands](#the-islands).

Our homes are in unincorporated Maricopa County. We do not vote in Peoria city elections.

## How we work
- We are run by volunteers who live here.
- We listen first, then act.
- We are nonpartisan. Everyone in the islands is welcome.
- We share what we learn in plain language.

# Tax Reform

## City sales tax in the county islands

Many island residents see Peoria city sales tax on bills and online orders. [TPT RATE] [VERIFY]

But island homes are not in the city. We get no city services and no city vote.

### Questions residents are raising
- Why are island addresses charged a city tax?
- Is the address data used by sellers and the state correct for our homes?
- Where does this money go?
- How can it be fixed?

### What we are doing
- Gathering examples from residents' bills and receipts.
- Asking state and county officials for clear answers.
- Learning about options, with the help of qualified advisors.

### Share your example
Seen a city tax on your bill? [Send it to us](#contact). Please remove account numbers first.

*CIC does not give tax or legal advice.*

# Anti-Annexation

## Staying a County Island

We like where we live. Many of us chose the county on purpose.

We believe residents should decide their own future.

### Why it matters to us
- Our community character.
- Our property and our choices.
- Our voice in local decisions.

### What we stand for
- Island residents decide whether to change status. Not anyone else.
- Services like fire protection should not depend on joining a city.
- Open, honest information about any annexation proposal.

### Stay alert
If you get mail or a visit about annexation, [let us know](#contact).
